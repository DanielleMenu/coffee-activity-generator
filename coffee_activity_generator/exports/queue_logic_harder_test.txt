Queue Logic Puzzle

Patrons:
- Olive
- Diego
- Willow
- Jules

Orders:
- Decaf Caramel Cold Brew
- Decaf Macchiato
- Extra Shot Hazelnut Cold Brew
- Extra Hot Cappuccino

Clues:
1. The second customer to arrive ordered the Decaf Macchiato.
2. The last drink prepared belonged to the customer who ordered Decaf Macchiato.
3. Diego arrived before Olive.
4. Olive arrived before Jules.
5. Olive's drink was prepared before Jules's.
6. Willow's drink was prepared before Olive's.
7. The Decaf Caramel Cold Brew was prepared before the Extra Shot Hazelnut Cold Brew.
8. The Extra Shot Hazelnut Cold Brew was prepared before the Extra Hot Cappuccino.
